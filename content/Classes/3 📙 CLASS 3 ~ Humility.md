### FULL SCRIPT – CLASS #3

The third "Class" in this "Course" is what I refer to as "Humility."

"Humility" is also very simple. It’s a natural extension of the first definition of “Nice,” which is: It’s best to think of others, not just yourself, and that includes the natural world.

Humility is being able to empathize with others and trying to view things from their point of view. It comes naturally when one understands and accepts that they are not better or more important than anyone else — and that the world is complex and filled with people who have their own perspectives and experiences. Humility leads to empathy.

Since nations are the most powerful man-made entities on planet Earth — and they’re not going anywhere anytime soon — the concept of humility has profound implications in international relations and global power dynamics.

### Mearsheimer and the Role of Threat

Mearsheimer said politics is a contact sport and that war will remain on the table.

Yes, but: if we root arguments in logic and rationality, we lower the temperature of political discourse.

War itself is the problem — not the threat of war. The threat should be used to prevent war. That’s the core of the Handsome Bouncer concept.
